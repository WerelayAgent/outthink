Privacy Notice

What OutThink AI stores, why it stores it, and what never leaves your device or the vault.

Wallet identity

To submit or review a case you connect a wallet and sign a one-time message. We store the public wallet address, the signature check result and the profile details you enter (name, profession, email). We never ask for, see or store your private key or seed phrase.

Case content

Case text is scanned for personal and health information before it is stored. Raw case text is kept in the private vault and is not published. Only a content hash, the profession, aggregate difficulty statistics and the author's public profile are shown publicly.

Attachments

Uploaded files are stored privately and can only be downloaded by the wallet that uploaded them or by an administrator. Each file is fingerprinted with SHA-256 and a PII scan result is recorded with it.

AI processing

Case prompts and questions are sent to third-party model providers through OpenRouter so the panel can answer and grade them. The expert answer is sent to the grading model with the rubric. If you do not want a case processed by external models, do not submit it.

Demo data

Some showcase rows are seeded demo content, marked as demo in the interface and counted separately from real submissions. They are illustrative and not real model output.

Your control

Disconnect your wallet at any time. To request removal of a submitted case or profile, contact us through the channel on the contact page.
