Terms of Use

The rules for submitting cases, reviewing them, and licensing the resulting dataset.

What OutThink AI is

OutThink AI is an arena where licensed professionals submit real work cases, a panel of AI models attempts them, and verified peer reviewers decide whether the case is valid. Verified cases can be licensed to AI labs as evaluation data.

Your submissions

  • You must own the case and have the right to share it.
  • You must remove personal, patient and client identifying information. Automated and manual checks run, but you remain responsible.
  • One case per wallet per title or content. Duplicate submissions are rejected.
  • Tier 0 accounts lock a refundable anti-spam stake when submitting.

Reviews

Reviewers must have a verified profession tier, cannot review their own case, and cannot review the same case twice. Review accuracy affects reputation.

Dataset licensing

Licences are granted only after a human review of the request. Case content is never published publicly, and every export carries a buyer-specific watermark so leaks can be traced.

Token naming

$OUTTHINK is a working name for a planned token. No token sale is live, nothing on this site is an offer or solicitation to buy any token, and no return is promised. Contract addresses will be published only after deployment.

AI output

Model answers and grades come from third-party providers and can be wrong. The expert answer and reviewer consensus, not the model output, define a case's correctness.

Demo content

Seeded showcase rows are marked as demo and are excluded from real statistics. They are illustrative only.
